THE MAKERS · 2026 DRAFT

So… who actually won the draft?

Yahoo gets a vote. It does not get the final vote. We graded the finished roster, keeper price, positional balance, depth, late-round upside and the actual cost paid.

Our winner: Chris — Criterus, A+. Yahoo gave him a C+. The deepest roster in the room is also the first major algorithm fight of the season.

Best No-Keeper Build

Nate

Most Dangerous Omission

Nick · no tight end drafted

Nick leaves the draft with two quarterbacks and zero tight ends. It is fixable, but the waiver wire has an assignment before Week 1.

Most Dangerous Omission II

Max · QB pushed to Round 15

Waiting on quarterback can be smart. Waiting until Malik Willis at 15.8 means the plan now requires a successful post-draft add.

#1A+
Chris · YAHOO C+

Criterus

Why it works: The first eight rounds are relentless. Taylor/Jefferson/Nabers/Bucky gives Chris four weekly anchors before Hurts, Harrison and Pitts even enter the picture. Skattebo at keeper cost makes the running-back depth borderline obnoxious.

What can go wrong: There is only one keeper discount and Chris spends a lot of late capital adding even more running backs. If the top receivers miss time, the bench is more backfield warehouse than balanced emergency kit.
